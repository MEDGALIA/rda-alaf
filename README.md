# RDA-ALAF
## Agentic Landscape Assessment Framework

RDA-ALAF (Agentic Landscape Assessment Framework) is an open-source framework
for guiding agentic approaches to research data analysis and data sharing using
structured knowledge and current-event intelligence.

The framework will use GitOps and agentic workflows to help maintain and update
its knowledge base and support the development and evolution of the framework.
Agents may identify relevant changes, propose updates, and provide guidance,
but changes to the framework and knowledge base will require human review and
approval.

Human experts, including members of the research data community, remain in the
loop to validate agent-proposed changes and maintain the quality, relevance,
and provenance of the framework and its underlying knowledge.

## Status

**Early development / experimental**

RDA-ALAF is currently being developed as an open-source project in alignment
with proposed activities around the Research Data Alliance (RDA).

The project is not an official RDA output, recommendation, or RDA Working Group
repository unless and until formally designated as such through the appropriate
RDA processes.

A proposed RDA Working Group, VANTAGE, is exploring related concepts and
activities. RDA-ALAF is intended to provide a technical foundation that may
support such work if the Working Group is formally established and adopts or
builds upon the framework.

## Objectives

RDA-ALAF aims to provide a lightweight and extensible framework for:

- Guiding agentic approaches to research data analysis and data sharing.
- Maintaining structured, machine-readable knowledge about relevant technologies, practices, and developments.
- Incorporating current-event intelligence to keep knowledge relevant as the research data landscape evolves.
- Using GitOps and agentic workflows to propose and manage updates to the framework and its knowledge base.
- Keeping humans in the loop, with proposed changes requiring human review and approval before they become part of the framework or knowledge base.
- Supporting transparent, reproducible, and community-informed development.

## Architecture

The framework is designed around several complementary components:

- **Structured Knowledge Base** — Curated, machine-readable information that provides the foundation for agentic analysis and guidance.
- **Current-Event Intelligence** — Information about emerging developments, technologies, and practices that can inform proposed updates to the knowledge base.
- **Agentic Workflows** — Agents that analyze available information, identify potential changes, and propose updates or improvements to the framework.
- **GitOps** — Version-controlled workflows for managing proposed changes, preserving provenance, and enabling reproducible review.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Review** — Human experts review and approve agent-proposed changes before they are incorporated into the framework or knowledge base.
- **Extensible Framework** — A modular foundation that can evolve as new research data needs, technologies, and agentic capabilities emerge.
